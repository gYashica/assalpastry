Our Heritage

A Taste of Persia
in Every Bite

Assal Pastry has been Irvine's beloved destination for authentic Persian and Middle Eastern sweets since 1989. Our pastries are crafted using time-honored family recipes passed down through generations — the same recipes that have delighted guests in Tehran, Isfahan, and beyond.

Every treat is made fresh each morning using premium ingredients: pure saffron, rose water, cardamom, pistachios, and wildflower honey. No shortcuts. No artificial flavors. Just the real thing, made with care.

Whether you're discovering Persian sweets for the first time or seeking a familiar taste of home, Assal Pastry welcomes you.
